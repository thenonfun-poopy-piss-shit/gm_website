gm_reallifespa was a map in production around October 2021, by me. The map was meant to be a recreation of the Spa Bomba in Nurmes Finland, specifically the pools of that.

Quickly after starting making the map I realized that I was way too unskilled for a map like this. With all the curvature of the pools and other stuff, the map never came along.

The map in its current state is very clearly unfinished, with basically nothing done on it.

In 2023 shortly after CS2's launch, I tried remaking it in CS2's engine, however that also didn't go well, and is completely lost by now.

The newest BSP of the map has the filename of gm_reallifesuperduperspasuperwhyisthisnamesolonglikewtf.bsp
