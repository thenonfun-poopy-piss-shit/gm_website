gm_hotelmaze was a map in production around March 2023, by me and panman. Gm_hotelmaze was meant to be the "Sequel" to gm_officemaze.

The map was set in a "Hotel" at night. The hotel consists of a few floors, a food restaurant, kitchen, a basement, and that's basically it.

This map just never got off the ground due to the map layout really not being that good, and just in general no ideas on how to continue it.
