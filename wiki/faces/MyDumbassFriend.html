My Dumbass Friend

My Dumbass Friend
The face of The Fun, as seen in the Garry's Mod addon.

My Dumbass Friend is a photo of The Fun. This photo was used in the Garry's Mod workshop addon my dumbass friend as a fast zombie reskin by Steam user panman released on October 4th, 2024. The mod itself is a reskin of the Fast Zombie from the 2004 Video Game Half-Life 2

History

This image of The Fun was taken on the 16th of August, 2024, The image itself was taken on a Google Pixel 7a Smartphone with its Main camera. The mod itself had been made some time prior to its release, However wasnt going to be released until The Fun requested that it would be. The Fun contributed nothing else to the mods making except for the Picture of his Face, all other work was done by panman

Reception

Upon the mods release, the mod was met with questions on why it had gotten to the front page, such as the comment "why is flawg on the front page" posted by Steam user Murasaki only after a few minutes after the mods release, Though most else of the reception was Positive. There were a few people who thought that the mod was doing a Doxx on The Fun, comment posted by Steam user Darkness states "0/10, doxxes a guy". A following responce was written by mod creator panman a few minutes later, which reads as "who "he"? what doxxing even? my friend showed his face on a live stream on his youtube channel and i did ask him for permission to upload this reskin, i did credit him, so stop saying stuff like this iut of the blue without thinking." After this, the Workshop pages description was changed to have the following disclaimer at the end of it: "I SHOULD MENTION THAT MY FRIEND IS OK WITH THIS UPLOAD, I UPLOADED THIS ADDON UNFER HIS PERMISSION AND THE NAME WAS MADE BY HIM, NO DOXXING OR BULLYING WAS DONE IN THE MAKING, TESTING AND UPLOADING OF THIS ADDON. The mod has achieved Five stars on the Steam Workshop, along with 36,882 Visitors and 3,384 current subscribers as of 2026.
