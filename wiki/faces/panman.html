panman

panman
Profile picture of panman from 2023

panman is a Ukrainian content creator who makes Source Engine content, also creator of his own personal site panman.neocities.org. panman's birthday is on the 30th of June, 2008, making panman 18 years old as of June 2026. He has been creating Source Engine content since 2021.

panman is well known for making Garry's Mod playermodels and maps, particularly in the style of Half-Life 2. He is also known for creating the Garry's Mod addon called "my dumbass friend as a fast zombie reskin" and helping with the creation of gm_wayremake.
