Fat Dad

Fat Dad
The face of the late Bill Yaryan, as known as "Fat Dad" here

Fat Dad is a photo of Bill Yaryan, which became an inside joke between panman and The Fun. It was later used as clan icon of the Fat Dad Clan in the game Counter Strike:Source.

Contents
  • History
  • See also

History

This image of Bill Yaryan was taken sometime in 2001 and was sourced from the Ultimate Beer Gut Contest 2001 from dayoopers.com. In 2022, the image appeared on a random TF2 themed Telegram group chat panman was in, and sometime in 2025 panman rediscovered the image again and added it to a collection of images called "russian people images" despite Bill not being russian, all because the image is rather comical, thus earning it's title as "Fat Dad". Bill Yaryan sadly passed away in 2024.
