REDACTED.com

REDACTED.com
Album cover of REDACTED.com

REDACTED.com is an album by panman, The Fun, and Snazcat that was fully released on the 20th of July, 2026. The album is a parody of real music leaks. The album was presented as a website that hosted said leaks in low quality. The full album was then released on lossless on bandcamp.

Creation

REDACTED.com started from The Fun sending panman the song "Boulangerie" by Jeremy Sherman, and claiming it was a new "leaked" Kendrick Lamar song. panman then decided to mashup the song with "TV OFF". After it was done, panman added in "(REDACTED.COM)" to the filename, as a joke acting as if was a real leak. After this panman made another REDACTED.com song, this time being a mix of Drakes "Family matters" with Kevin MacLeod "Cipher". On the 11th of July, 2026, The Fun and panman started work on the redacteddotcom.neocities.org site. The Fun during this time also made a few songs, being Kendricklex, Classy FACK, and Jennifers Tamale. Eventually Snazcat also made a few songs for the album. The songs were made in FL Studio (panman, Snazcat) and in Mixcraft 9 (The Fun).

The Site

REDACTED.com
Screenshot of the redacteddotcom.neocities.org index.

The site features each song of the album hosted in extremely low quality, with an option to download them. There is also a VIP section that hosts things like a Minecraft skin(in high quality) and extra songs from the album. along with the Bandcamp link for the album, However to get these VIP perks you need to get a code. You get this code by emailing the gmail adress linked on the page. The site was made for Internet Explorer 11 Compatibility like gm_website was.

Site Takedown & Reinstation

The site was up until the 19th of July, when it randomly went down without any reason given from neocities. An email was sent to neocities support during this time, however they only responded after the site was brought back up. The site randomly came back up on the 23rd, and Neocities responded on the 25th.
