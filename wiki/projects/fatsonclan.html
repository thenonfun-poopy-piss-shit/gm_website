Fat Son Clan

Fat Son Clan
Fat Son Clan's icon

Fat Son Clan is a Steam group based on a "Fat son". The group mainly plays the game Counter Strike:Source. The group was created by FearDark on the 28th of July 2026. The clan has a rivalry against FAT DAD CLAN. Notable players in Fat Son Clan are: FearDark, Dante, Youcef, and Eliasoff.
