gm_center was a map in production around February 2023, by me. gm_center was meant to be the real sequel to WAY.

This however never happened, instead gm_wayremake began development in October of 2023, and that instead was the sequel to WAY.

The map didn't follow the same layout of WAY at all, and was meant to be its own thing.
